> Fictional contract created solely for the Promise Ledger demonstration.

## 7.2 Deletion and backup retention

Following termination of the Services, Tasklane will delete Northwind's customer personal data within 30 days. Deletion includes application records, audit events, exports, attachments, and backups controlled by Tasklane.

Database snapshots and other retained backups must not remain available beyond the same 30-day period.

## 9.1 Data residency

Customer personal data belonging to Northwind's European users will be stored and processed only in `eu-west-1`.

## 11.3 Subprocessor notice

Tasklane will notify Northwind at least 30 days before adding a new subprocessor and will maintain a current subprocessor list available to Northwind.
