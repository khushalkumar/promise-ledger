# Acme Master Services Agreement 2026

> Fictional contract created solely for the Promise Ledger demonstration.

## 4.1 Business plan features

Tasklane will make SSO configuration available to administrators of every Business plan workspace.

## 6.2 Availability

Tasklane will maintain 99.9% monthly service availability. If availability falls below 99.9%, eligible customers may request service credits under the published service-level schedule.

## 8.1 Security controls

Tasklane will encrypt customer data at rest and require multi-factor authentication for administrative accounts.

## 10.4 Termination

On termination, Tasklane will return or delete customer content as required by the applicable data processing addendum.
