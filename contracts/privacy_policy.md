# Tasklane Privacy Policy

> Fictional policy created solely for the Promise Ledger demonstration.

## 5. Data retention

After an account is closed, Tasklane may retain customer personal data and backups for up to 90 days before permanent deletion.

This public-policy statement intentionally conflicts with the 30-day deletion commitment in Northwind DPA v3 so Promise Ledger can demonstrate cross-document contradiction detection.
